How it works

Pick a dandelion, daisy or grass and draw a stem upwards in the sky. A stem always grows out of the ground and stops at the height line; when you lift your finger it bursts. A tap grows a straight stem to the height line for you. A tap above the height line always launches a dandelion rocket, whatever is picked, and it bursts where you tapped, so with grass picked you can play streamers below the line and rockets above it.

Each plant is its own firework. The dandelion is a rocket: it whistles higher as it climbs, trailing sparks, then bangs and blows out into seeds that stall, turn their parachutes up and drift away. The daisy opens as a ring of petals around a gold centre with a chime pitched by where it stands, low on the left and high on the right. Grass is a streamer: it never bursts, it just streaks up the sky fizzing, crackling and shedding glitter.

Every stroke is recorded in order, with its timing. Replay plays the sequence back as you drew it (pauses longer than 4 seconds are shortened). Play on beat puts one burst on every so many beats at the tempo you set: press it on a beat of the song and burst 1 lands one interval later. Rise is how many beats a stem spends growing and whistling first, so the bang lands on the beat rather than starting from it.

Tap the Tap button along with the song to measure its BPM. Click track plays a metronome so you can check the match. Loop keeps the sequence going until Stop.

While a sequence is playing, anything you draw goes off live on top of it but isn't recorded, so you can lay down a beat, loop it, and jam over it. Stop to go back to recording.

On an iPhone, turn off silent mode to hear the sounds. Your sequence is kept in this browser between visits.

How it works

Pick Stone and draw the outline of a stone on the pad; a tap makes a pebble. It drops into the garden from above and lands with a thud. Pick Rake path and draw where the monk should drive the Zamboni. Its rake combs grooves into the sand behind it, and where the path runs into a stone the Zamboni goes round it instead: Laps times in a widening spiral the first time it meets each stone, which leaves the rings a garden has around its rocks, then on round the stone until it can pick the path up again on the far side. Gap is how close it dares to drive to a stone.

Replay rebuilds the garden in the order you drew it: the stones fall one per beat at the Tempo, then the Zamboni rakes each path in turn, moving Speed rake widths every beat. Press Replay on a beat: the first stone lands one beat later. Tap the Tap button along with a song to measure its tempo.

Changing the rake, laps or gap redraws the finished garden. Your drawing is kept in this browser between visits.

How it works

Draw in the white box. Every stroke is kept as a line, not pixels, so the drawing is re-inked at the output's own resolution and the motes pick their resting places from the inked area.

Until you draw anything, the guide text stands in as the shape. The guide is only a tracing aid: as soon as there is a stroke on the pad, the motes follow your strokes instead.

The motes are fireflies: a mote only shows while it moves, and it glows brighter the faster it goes. A mote at rest is invisible.

Gather is the share of motes drawn into the drawing. Once there they keep crawling up and down inside the strokes, and that motion is what traces the shape. Set Crawl to 0 and the character goes dark as soon as it forms. The other motes drift on a slow current that Swarm speeds up. Cycle runs swarm → gather → hold → release; set it to 0 to let the shape form once and stay.

Breath makes the crawling motes stop and start, one full breath every so many seconds. Its Shape decides where the rest falls. A wave sends bands of rest across the drawing. Whole stops and starts the entire drawing at once. A spiral splits the drawing into a resting half and a moving half that curl into each other and turn around the centre; Twist bends the line between them, and Seeds sets the size of the small patch of each half that sits inside the other.

Speed sets a mote's colour. A fast mote takes the Moving colour, a still one the Still colour, and the Pivot is the speed in between where a mote vanishes into the ground. At pivot 0 only moving motes show. Strays dims the motes that never gather.

Stages load a colour and motion setting, and each stage keeps its own drawing. 無極 is light on white with no distinction yet. In 太極 movement gives rise to yang and stillness to yin (太極動而生陽，靜而生陰): the whole drawing moves and glows light, then rests and turns dark, one motion and one stillness, each the root of the other (一動一靜，互為其根). Yin and yang are still moments in time, not places. In 陰陽 they divide and stand side by side (分陰分陽，兩儀立焉): a moving half and a resting half, always equal, turn around the centre, each carrying a seed of the other.

Play on beat loosens each stroke's motes into a faint haze around it and condenses your strokes back one at a time, in the order you drew them, one stroke every so many beats at the tempo you set. Tap the Tap button along with the song to measure its BPM. Press Play on a beat: the first stroke lands one interval later. Replay stops it.

White motes on a near-white ground are meant to be barely there. To see what they are doing, turn the mote colour dark for a moment.
